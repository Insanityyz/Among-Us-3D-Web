A fan-made, first-person 3D tribute to the social deduction phenomenon.
Find the impostor. Finish your tasks. Don't get spaced.

Crewmates: complete every task on your list, or vote out all impostors. Move with WASD, look with the mouse, press E to interact, R to report a body, Tab for the map.

Impostors: kill the crew with Q, travel through vents with V, and call sabotages with Tab. Blend in — fake your tasks.

Touch controls appear automatically on mobile.
